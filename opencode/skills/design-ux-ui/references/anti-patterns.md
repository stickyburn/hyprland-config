# Generic Output Diagnostic

Use on a draft or for a design critique. Diagnose defaults rather than banning
entire styles. Replacing a violet gradient with cream and a serif can leave
the same generic composition underneath.

## Specific Habits to Check

Unless the brief or existing brand justifies them, replace habitual:

- centered marketing heroes and three equal icon cards on unrelated surfaces
- nested cards, excessive pills, and decorative accent rails
- cream/terracotta/serif, violet/glass/glow, or black/neon packages chosen by habit
- italic accent words, meaningless “01/02/03” labels, and tiny uppercase mono
- background orbs, floating blobs, and entrance animations on every section
- button/card lift or scale on every hover; everyday controls should stay stable
- oversized metrics, vague headings, and unsourced social proof as space fillers

Do not replace these with a new mandatory aesthetic. Preserve justified brand
choices and seek a concept rooted in this product's content and task.

## Repair the Highest-Level Cause

| Symptom | Repair |
|---|---|
| App looks like a marketing page | Recompose around the working task and state |
| Every region has equal emphasis | Prioritize, group, compare, or subordinate |
| Cards inside cards, repeated borders/padding | Flatten; keep containers with actual meaning |
| Unclear action or toast-only form errors | Repair interaction, labels, and recovery |
| Trend tokens without identity | Revisit type, palette, imagery, and signature |
| Glow, blur, or motion without a spatial/state reason | Remove or tie to an intentional model |
| Invented claims or ornamental data | Replace with truthful content or labeled sample data |

One well-developed signature with supporting craft is stronger than many
unrelated effects. For visual-first briefs, subtraction alone is not enough:
develop a memorable composition, asset treatment, or useful visual instrument.

## Inspect the Rendered Draft

1. Without effects, is the task and hierarchy still legible?
2. Would the design fit an unrelated product after changing only its name?
3. Where does the subject first become unmistakable?
4. Does each conspicuous device carry content, state, or identity?
5. Do typography, media, spacing, and interaction sustain the quality of the
   focal region, including at narrow widths and in non-happy states?

Use screenshots when available; source code alone cannot establish visual
quality. Never claim access to a screen you did not inspect.

## Critique Language

Order findings by user impact. State evidence, consequence, concrete repair,
and a meaningful tradeoff; distinguish defects from taste. Avoid unexplained
“cleaner,” “modern,” or “premium.”

Example: “Suspended and active accounts use identical cards, hiding urgent
state during scanning. Keep active accounts in the table and expose suspended
ones in a warning row. This improves triage but adds a visual exception.”
